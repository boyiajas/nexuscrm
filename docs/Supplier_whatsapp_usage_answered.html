Overview & Purpose

1. Please describe the business purpose for using WhatsApp to communicate with customers.
WhatsApp is used as a controlled customer communication channel for collections and account follow-up workflows. The platform supports outbound campaign messaging, approved WhatsApp templates, inbound customer replies, and optional live-chat escalation so authorised staff can contact debtors/customers regarding collections, reminders, and service follow-up in an auditable system rather than unmanaged personal messaging.

3. Is WhatsApp the primary customer interaction channel or a supporting channel to another platform?
Supporting channel. The platform is a CRM with multi-channel capability (WhatsApp, email, SMS), and WhatsApp is one supported engagement path rather than the only customer interaction platform.
4. Which customer journey stages involve WhatsApp (e.g. application, collections, service queries)?
  • Collections and debt follow-up campaigns
  • Reminder and notification messaging
  • Customer reply capture and response tracking
  • Service queries / live chat once a customer replies
  • Escalation from automated flow/template messaging to agent-assisted conversation

  • Customer name, phone, and where available email address

  • Delivery status, provider message SID, error code, error message, delivery timestamp
  • Inbound reply content and reply timestamps
  • Live chat session and chat message content where live chat is used
  • Audit trail entries for relevant actions
